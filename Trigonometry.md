## Radian:

- 1 Radian is the measure of an angle subtended at the centre of a circle by an arc equal in length to the radius. $180^\circ=\pi^c$
- **Arc length:** $l=r\theta$, where $r$ is the radius, and $\theta$ is the angle subtended by the arc in radians.

## Unit Circle:

- Unit circle is a circle with center at origin $O(0,0)$ and radius 1 unit.
- For any point $P(x,y)$ on the unit circle, $x=\cos\theta$, and $y=\sin\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle that segment $PO$ makes with the positive X-axis.
- Sign of trig ratios in quadrants.

  |       | Q1        | Q2        | Q3        | Q4        |
  | ----- | --------- | --------- | --------- | --------- |
  | Signs | All $+ve$ | $\sin+ve$ | $\tan+ve$ | $\cos+ve$ |

## Trigonometric Ratios:

| $\theta$     | $0$ | $30^\circ$/$\dfrac{\pi}{6}^c$ | $45^\circ$/$\dfrac{\pi}{4}^c$ | $60^\circ$/$\dfrac{\pi}{3}^c$ | $90^\circ$/$\dfrac{\pi}{2}^c$ |
| ------------ | --- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- |

## Identities:

- $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$
- Divinding by $\cos^2\theta$, we get $1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$
- Divinding by $\sin^2\theta$, we get $1+\cot^2\theta=\cosec^2\theta$

## Properties of triangles:

- Addition of lengths of any 2 sides is always greater than the 3rd side.
- **Sine Rule:** $\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}=\dfrac{c}{\sin C}=k$, where $A,B,C$ are the angles of the triangle, $a,b,c$ are the lengths of the sides opposite those angles, and $k$ is a constant.
- **Cosine Rule:** $\cos A=\dfrac{b^2+c^2-a^2}{2bc}$ $\therefore a^2 = b^2+c^2-2bc\cos(A)$
- **Semiperimeter:** Half of sum of side lengths: <br> $s=\dfrac{a+b+c}{2}$
- **Area:** $\Delta = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$
- **Area of equilateral triangle:** $\Delta = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}a^2}{4}$
